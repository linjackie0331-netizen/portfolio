20%+ YoY Revenue Growth ×3: Annual Business Plan Architecture Across 5 Concurrent Brands

Situation

Five premium consumer brands (iRobot, Spärkel, SteamOne, Blueair, Cuckoo) required simultaneous annual marketing plans with a single NT$20M+ shared A&P pool; team of 5 was structured as brand generalists, creating execution gaps and unclear accountability.

Action

Restructured team from brand-based to function-based specialist roles; introduced OKR coaching cycle so each member’s ownership, KPI, and budget responsibility was pre-aligned before execution started; built cross-brand annual plan with priority matrix, ROI-gated budget allocation, and quarterly milestone reviews.

ROAS 6x + Category #1: Demand-Validated Zero-to-Launch in an Undefined Category
